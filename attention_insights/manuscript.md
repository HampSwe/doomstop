Read this aloud at a normal pace. Don't read the headings, and take a short breath

## 1. Opening story

The night before my biggest exam, I skipped sleep. The next morning my mind was
completely blank.

## 2. Definitions and numbers

Sleep has four stages. Stage two makes up about fifty percent of the night,
and a full cycle lasts roughly ninety minutes.

## 3. Surprising result

But here's the surprise: people who sleep after practicing piano wake up playing
better, without any extra practice.
